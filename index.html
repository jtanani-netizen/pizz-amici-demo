Story

A fresh take on a city classic

“Whether you call it 'thin crust' or 'tavern style', we can all agree that it's the preferred style of the true Chicagoan… our pizzas are cracker-thin, flavorful, and made with the care of old-school tradition” (official site). Opened November 15, 2024 by Billy and Cecily Federighi — and booked weeks ahead ever since (OpenTable).
